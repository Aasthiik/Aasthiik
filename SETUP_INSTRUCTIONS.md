## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Images not showing?**
- Wait 5-10 minutes for cached images to refresh
- Hard refresh your profile page (Ctrl+Shift+R)

**Links broken?**
- Replace `Aasthiik` with your actual GitHub username
- Verify all URLs are correct

**Stats not appearing?**
- Some stats services have rate limits
- They'll load within 24 hours
- Check if your profile is public
